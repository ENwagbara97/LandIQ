Here is a brief, step-by-step overview of how the LandIQ system functions under the hood:

**1. Data Intake (The Inputs)**
The system accepts land data through multiple channels:
*   **Manual Entry:** Typing in surveyor coordinates (Eastings/Northings or Latitude/Longitude).
*   **Document Uploads:** Uploading PDF surveys, CAD files, or images. LandIQ uses AI (Vision and Document Parsing agents) to read these files and extract the boundary coordinates automatically.
*   **Map Drawing:** Users can manually draw a polygon directly on the map interface.

**2. The Processing Pipeline (The Agents)**
Once coordinates are gathered, LandIQ routes the data through specialized Python "agents" to perform automated checks:
*   **`coord_extract.py` & `tabular_parser.py`:** Cleans up messy data, fixes swapped Eastings/Northings, and standardizes the land polygon.
*   **`gis_analysis.py`:** Calculates the exact land area, generates the terrain/elevation profile, and plots the exact perimeter on the map.
*   **`risk_assess.py`:** Cross-references the land boundaries against geographical hazard layers (like known flood plains or protected government zones).

**3. Title & Registry Verification**
The system checks the extracted location and boundaries against a local cadastral registry (handled by scripts in `db/` and `core/adapters.py`). This verifies if the land title is valid, if there are known disputes, or if the land is illegally encroaching on government property. 

**4. Scoring & Reporting (The Output)**
Finally, LandIQ aggregates all this data and calculates a **Viability Index** (e.g., 82%). 
It packages the results into the **Buyer's Decision Card** on the UI, grading specific metrics like:
*   Flood Risk
*   Terrain & Slope
*   Road Access
*   Encroachment & Title Status

It then issues a final "Traffic Light" recommendation (Green for Proceed, Amber for Caution, Red for Reject) and allows the user to export a formal PDF report. Everything is designed to run securely and locally on your machine.
